Refund & Returns Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

We want you to love how you walk. If something isn't right, here's how returns, exchanges, and refunds work.

Our 30-day promise

You may return or exchange eligible items within 30 days of delivery. We offer free size exchanges within this window, so you can get the perfect fit.

Condition of returns

To be eligible, items must be:

  • Unworn, unwashed, and in their original condition;
  • Returned with all original packaging, dust bags, and tags intact;
  • Free from scuffs, marks, or signs of wear (please try shoes on a soft, clean surface).

How to start a return or exchange

  1. Email support@aurastepsusa.com with your order number and whether you'd like a refund or an exchange.
  2. We'll reply with return instructions and the return address.
  3. Pack the item securely and send it back within the 30-day window.

Return shipping

For size exchanges, we cover the cost. For other returns, the cost of return shipping is the customer's responsibility, unless the item arrived faulty or incorrect — in which case we'll cover it in full.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll email you to confirm. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. For card payments, the time it takes to appear on your statement depends on your bank.

For crypto payments, refunds are issued in the same cryptocurrency to a wallet address you provide, at the coin amount originally received.

Non-returnable items

For hygiene and safety reasons, we can't accept returns of worn items or items returned outside the 30-day window. Sale or clearance items, if any, will be marked as final sale at the time of purchase.

Faulty or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged, faulty, or not as described, contact us within 7 days of delivery with a photo and we'll make it right — a replacement or a full refund, including shipping.

Questions?

We're here to help — email support@aurastepsusa.com.
