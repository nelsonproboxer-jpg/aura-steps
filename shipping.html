Shipping Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Where we ship

Aura Steps ships worldwide. Choose your country at checkout and we'll deliver to your door, with prices shown in your local currency.

Processing time

Orders are prepared and dispatched within 2–4 business days of payment being confirmed. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays begin processing the next business day. Crypto orders are dispatched once the payment is confirmed on-chain.

Delivery estimates

  • United States: typically 3–7 business days after dispatch.
  • United Kingdom & Europe: typically 5–10 business days after dispatch.
  • Rest of the world: typically 7–20 business days after dispatch, depending on destination and customs.

These are estimates, not guarantees — delivery can occasionally take longer due to carrier delays, customs, or peak periods.

Shipping cost

  • Free shipping on all orders over $500.
  • A flat rate of $40 applies to orders under that amount.

Tracking

Once your order ships, we'll email you a confirmation. If a tracking number is available for your shipment, it will be included in that email.

Customs & duties

For deliveries that cross a border, any import duties, taxes, or customs fees are set by the destination country and are the responsibility of the recipient. We're unable to predict or control these charges.

Wrong or undeliverable addresses

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If a parcel is returned to us as undeliverable due to an incorrect address, we'll reach out to arrange re-shipment (additional shipping may apply).

Questions?

Email us any time at support@aurastepsusa.com and we'll be glad to help.
