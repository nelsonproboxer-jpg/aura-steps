Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Aura Steps ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit aurastepsusa.com or place an order, and how we use and protect it.

Information we collect

  • Order & contact details you provide: name, email address, shipping address, and phone number.
  • Payment information: card payments are processed securely by our payment provider (Flutterwave). We never see or store your full card number or CVV. Crypto payments are made directly to our wallet addresses.
  • Usage data: basic information about how you use the site, collected via cookies and analytics/advertising tools (see below).

How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders and send order confirmations;
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide customer support;
  • To improve our site and products;
  • To measure and improve our advertising, and to show you relevant ads.

Cookies & advertising tools

We use cookies and similar technologies, including advertising and analytics pixels from Meta (Facebook/Instagram), TikTok, and Google, to understand how visitors use our site and to measure and improve our ad campaigns. These tools may set cookies that help us and these platforms recognise your device across sites. You can control cookies through your browser settings, and opt out of personalised advertising through each platform's ad settings.

Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers that help us run the store, including:

  • Flutterwave — payment processing;
  • Resend — sending order-confirmation emails;
  • Geoapify — address autocomplete at checkout;
  • GitHub Pages / Cloudflare — website hosting and delivery;
  • Meta, TikTok, and Google — advertising and analytics.

We do not sell your personal information.

Data retention

We keep order information for as long as needed to fulfil your order, provide support, and meet legal or accounting requirements.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. To make a request, email us at support@aurastepsusa.com.
